Attention-Driven Gaussian Modeling for Total Duration of Heterogeneous Operations 2025

Case study: trailer loading with multi-SKU shipments.

Two-stream encode–attend–fuse architecture that ingests global operation features and per-item (sub-operation) features, and predicts both a baseline duration and its uncertainty σ with a heteroskedastic Gaussian head.

Trained using Gaussian NLL plus median-quantile (τ=0.5) and slope-penalty terms to handle rounding spikes, outliers, and distribution skew.
